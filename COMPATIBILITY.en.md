# Game compatibility

Every engine module from the tables is compiled into the app. A title earns the tested mark after being checked on a real device; what exactly has been reworked is visible in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.en.md).

Current statuses also live in `app/src/main/assets/engines.json` and are shown on the in-app **Engines** screen.

Русская версия: [COMPATIBILITY.md](COMPATIBILITY.md)
